Jam Squares

Jam squares (often referred to in South Africa as krummeltert or Hungarian tart) are a classic, nostalgic baked treat featuring a buttery shortbread-style crust topped with a sweet, sticky layer of fruit jam. They are traditionally finished by grating a portion of chilled dough over the jam filling, creating a golden, crumbly texture once baked.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups of Flour
  • 250g of Butter
  • 1/2 teaspoon of Salt
  • 1 cup (200g) White Sugar
  • 4 teaspoon(20ml) of Baking Powder
  • 1 cup smooth strawberry Jam
  • 2 Eggs

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, beat the softened butter and sugar until pale, soft and creamy.
  2. Add the eggs and mix until fully combined.
  3. In a separate medium bowl, stir together the plain flour, baking powder and salt.
  4. Add the dry ingredients to the butter mixture.
  5. Work the mixture together with your hands. It will look dry and crumbly at first, but it will come together into a stiff dough. Do not add liquid.
  6. Shape the dough into a cyclinder. Wrap it in cling film and chill in the fridge for at least 1 hour, or in the freezer for 30 minutes.
  7. Preheat oven to 180°C/356°F
  8. Line a 20cm x 20cm (8″ x 8″) square tin with baking paper, leaving a slight overhang.
  9. Cut three-quarters from the chilled dough log and place the remaining quarter back in the fridge.
  10. Grate the larger piece of dough into coarse crumbs.
  11. Spread the grated dough evenly into the prepared tin and press down very lightly.
  12. Warm the jam briefly until slightly runnier and easier to spread.
  13. Spoon the jam evenly over the crumb base.
  14. Grate the remaining chilled dough and sprinkle it evenly over the jam.
  15. Bake for 25-30 minutes, or until lightly golden and the jam is gently bubbling at the edges.
